## Your Markdown notes on Android

QOwnNotes for Android is a native, **offline-capable Markdown notes app** that
synchronizes with your own Nextcloud server. Read and edit your notes without
an internet connection, then synchronize your changes when the server is
reachable again.

It supports Markdown rendering and editing, favorites, sharing text from other
apps to create notes, and importing accounts from the **Nextcloud Files** Android
app through Single Sign-On.

Use it with [QOwnNotes for Nextcloud](/getting-started/qownnotes-nextcloud.md) to
access your notes across the desktop, Android, and the Nextcloud web interface.

## Get started

Open the [QOwnNotes F-Droid page](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/org.qownnotes.mobile/)
or search for **QOwnNotes** in your F-Droid client after updating its repositories.

GitHub releases remain available if you prefer downloading APKs directly or
keeping them up to date with **Obtainium**.

For installation details and Nextcloud account setup, see the
[QOwnNotes Android documentation](/getting-started/qownnotes-android.md).
