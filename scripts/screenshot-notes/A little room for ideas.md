# A little room for ideas

Your notes. Your files. Your own space.

QOwnNotes keeps everyday thoughts in plain-text Markdown,
ready to explore, connect, and make your own.

## Make space for what matters

- **Capture** an idea before it slips away
- **Connect** your research with links between notes
- **Create** something worth coming back to
